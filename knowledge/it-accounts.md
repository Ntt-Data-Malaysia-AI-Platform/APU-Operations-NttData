## Resetting a forgotten password
Use the self-service password portal from any browser: enter your staff or student ID, verify with your registered mobile number or personal email, then choose a new password. Passwords must be at least 12 characters with upper and lower case letters and a number. The new password works on email, Wi-Fi, the LMS and the portal within 5 minutes.

## Account locked out
Accounts lock for 15 minutes after 5 failed sign-in attempts. Wait 15 minutes, or reset your password through the self-service portal to unlock it straight away. If you are still locked out, call the IT Service Desk on extension 2100 or visit the help desk on Level 3 with your ID card.

## Multi-factor authentication (MFA)
All staff accounts require MFA through the Microsoft Authenticator app. If you change phones, register the new phone on the security info page before wiping the old one. Lost your phone? The IT Service Desk can issue a temporary access pass after checking your identity in person or by video call. IT staff will never ask for your password or MFA code.
