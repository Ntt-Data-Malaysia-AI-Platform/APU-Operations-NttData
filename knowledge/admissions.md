## Programmes and intakes
Undergraduate degrees in computing, engineering, business and design have intakes in January, March, May, July, September and November. Foundation programmes start every two months. Popular programmes include Computer Science, Software Engineering, Cyber Security, Data Science and Business Analytics, and Digital Marketing.

## Entry requirements (degree)
Typical requirements are STPM with 2 principal passes (minimum Grade C, GP 2.0), A-Level with 2 passes, UEC with 5 grade Bs, the APU Foundation with CGPA 2.0, or a related diploma with CGPA 2.5. Computing and engineering degrees also need a credit in Mathematics at SPM. International students need IELTS 5.0 or equivalent. The Dean may consider applicants below the published minimum case by case.

## Scholarships and fees
Merit scholarships cover 10% to 100% of tuition depending on results; sports and need-based awards are also available. A computing degree costs roughly RM 30,000 to RM 35,000 a year for Malaysian students. PTPTN loans are available for eligible Malaysians.

## Handling enquiries
Reply to new enquiries within 1 working day. Recommend at most two programmes that match the student's results and interests, explain the entry requirements and next intake, and invite them to an open day or info session. Follow up unanswered enquiries after 3 days and again after 7 days, then mark the lead inactive. Never promise admission or a scholarship.
