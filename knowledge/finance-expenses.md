## Submitting a claim
Submit expense claims in the finance system within 30 days of the expense, with itemised receipts. Your line manager approves the claim, and it is paid with the next monthly payroll. Claims without receipts are accepted only for amounts under RM 50 with a written explanation.

## Travel
Economy class is standard for flights under 8 hours. Taxi or e-hailing fares are claimable between the airport and the hotel or conference venue, and for work trips when public transport is impractical. Meals are claimable up to RM 60 a day for local travel and RM 120 a day for overseas travel. Overseas travel needs Dean or Director approval at least 4 weeks ahead.

## Not claimable
Alcohol, personal entertainment, traffic fines, upgrades for personal preference and expenses already paid by a sponsor are not claimable.
