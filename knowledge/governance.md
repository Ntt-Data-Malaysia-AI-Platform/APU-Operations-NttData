## Decision-making bodies
The Board of Directors sets strategy and approves the annual budget. The Senate is the highest academic body: it approves new programmes, major programme changes and academic regulations. The Management Committee, chaired by the Vice-Chancellor, runs day-to-day operations.

## Approving a new programme
A School proposes a new academic programme, the Faculty Academic Committee reviews it, and Senate approves it. It then goes to the Malaysian Qualifications Agency (MQA) for provisional accreditation before it can be advertised.

## Policy ownership
Every policy names an owner office and a review date, and is reviewed at least every 3 years. The Registrar's Office keeps the policy register and publishes approved versions on the staff intranet.
