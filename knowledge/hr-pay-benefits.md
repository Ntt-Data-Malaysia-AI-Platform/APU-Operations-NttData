## Payroll
Salaries are paid on the 25th of each month, or the previous working day if the 25th falls on a weekend or public holiday. Payslips are published on the HR portal 2 days before payday. Changes in net pay usually come from overtime claims, unpaid leave, a change in PCB tax deduction or an EPF contribution change; contact Payroll if a change is unexplained.

## Benefits
Staff are covered by group medical insurance with a panel of clinics and hospitals, an annual outpatient limit of RM 1,500, a dental and optical allowance of RM 500 a year, and group term life insurance. Staff and their children receive a tuition fee discount on APU programmes. EPF and SOCSO contributions follow statutory rates.

## Onboarding new staff
Before day one, HR issues the offer, IT creates accounts and prepares a laptop, and the line manager assigns a buddy. In week one: induction session, health and safety briefing, data protection (PDPA) training and system access checks. Within the first month, probation objectives are agreed with the line manager. Probation lasts 6 months, with a review at 3 months.

## Getting help
For sensitive matters such as grievances, misconduct or wellbeing concerns, contact your HR Business Partner directly. The Employee Assistance Programme offers free, confidential counselling.
