## Annual leave
Full-time staff receive 16 days of annual leave a year with less than 2 years of service, 18 days for 2 to 5 years, and 22 days for more than 5 years. Apply through the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance; your line manager approves. Up to 5 unused days can be carried forward and must be used by 31 March.

## Medical and hospitalisation leave
Staff are entitled to 14 days of paid medical leave a year (18 after 2 years, 22 after 5 years) and up to 60 days of hospitalisation leave. Upload the medical certificate to the HR portal within 2 working days.

## Other leave
Maternity leave is 98 days and paternity leave 7 days, in line with the Employment Act. Compassionate leave of 3 days applies on the death of an immediate family member. Unpaid leave needs Dean or Director approval.
