## Campus Wi-Fi
Connect to the APU-Secure network with your university email and password (PEAP, MSCHAPv2; most devices need no certificate validation). Guests use APU-Guest with a daily code from the front desk. If your device connects but has no internet, forget the network and reconnect, then check that the device's date and time are correct.

## VPN for remote access
Staff need the VPN to reach finance, HR and student-records systems from off campus. Install the GlobalProtect client from the software portal, connect to vpn.apu.example and sign in with MFA. Students do not need the VPN for the LMS or library databases.

## Requesting software
Standard software (Microsoft 365, Zoom, Python, VS Code) installs from the self-service software portal. Licensed software (Adobe Creative Cloud, MATLAB, SPSS, Tableau) needs a request with a business reason and your head of department's approval. Requests are usually fulfilled within 2 working days of approval.

## Laptop and device issues
For a slow or faulty university laptop, first restart it, install pending updates and check free disk space. The IT Service Desk repairs hardware faults, and a loan laptop is available when a repair takes longer than 1 day.
