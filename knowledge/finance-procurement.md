## Procurement thresholds
Up to RM 2,000: one quotation and head of department approval. RM 2,001 to RM 10,000: three written quotations, approved by the head of department and Finance. Above RM 10,000: formal tender or three quotations with a comparison sheet, approved by the Procurement Committee. Raise a purchase requisition in the finance system before placing any order. Splitting a purchase to stay under a threshold is not allowed.

## Invoices and payment terms
Suppliers are paid within 30 days of a valid invoice that matches an approved purchase order and a goods received note. Invoices go to the accounts payable mailbox. You can check an invoice's status in the finance system by invoice or PO number.

## Budgets
Each department receives an annual operating budget in January, and budget holders review monthly reports from Finance. Spending above 90% of budget, or moving money between budget lines, needs Finance approval. Unspent operating budget does not carry forward.
